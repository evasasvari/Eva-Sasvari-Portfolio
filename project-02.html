CASE STUDY 02

Little Trees Christmas Experience

Designing a seasonal digital experience around a strategic business opportunity.

Little Trees is a concept project exploring how an established brand could attract new customers through a Christmas-themed digital experience. Combining product strategy, UX/UI design, and branding, the project translates a seasonal opportunity into a cohesive product concept.

ROLE UX/UI Designer
DURATION 3 Weeks
FOCUS Product Strategy · UX/UI Design · Branding
#01 [THE OUTCOME]
A seasonal digital experience designed to engage new and existing customers.

The project resulted in a responsive website promoting a Christmas product collection, supported by refreshed branding elements and a user-centred browsing experience.

Rather than creating a traditional marketing campaign, the concept explores how a digital experience can strengthen emotional engagement while introducing the brand to a broader audience.

#02 [RESEARCH AND SETTING DIRECTION]
The audience, the brand, and how seasonal behaviour shapes the experience.

Research explored the current and the new target audience, the existing brand, and seasonal purchasing behaviour to better understand how users would discover and engage with the concept.

These insights helped define the value proposition, shape the communication style, and establish the overall structure and visual direction of the website.

#03 [IDENTIFYING THE OPPORTUNITY]
Identifying a realistic opportunity that creates value for users and the business.

Before designing the experience, I wanted to identify a realistic opportunity that could create value for both users and the business. Christmas became the foundation of the concept because it allows brands to build stronger emotional connections while naturally attracting new customer segments through seasonal products and gifting occasions.

Instead of selecting a theme arbitrarily, the concept was grounded in a clear business opportunity that guided the direction of the project.

#04 [BRINGING THE CONCEPT TO LIFE]

Subtle updates that add festive atmosphere while preserving the identity.

Alongside the website, I explored subtle updates to the visual identity that supported the seasonal concept, felt more inviting to the new target audience while preserving the recognisable Little Trees brand. Therefore the final solution includes storytelling, user guidance and a new visual product presentation.

#05 [TRANSLATING STRATEGY INTO UX]

Translating the strategy into a digital experience.

The strategic concept was translated into a digital experience through information architecture, user flows, and wireframes. The website was designed to guide visitors naturally from discovering the Christmas collection to exploring individual products, creating an intuitive experience across desktop and mobile devices.
