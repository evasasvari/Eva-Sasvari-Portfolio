#02 [ABOUT ME]

I design clear, human products from first sketch to shipped interface.

I like getting to the bottom of a problem, asking the right questions, and turning complexity into experiences that feel clear, intuitive, and purposeful.

My background has given me hands-on experience across the design process, from research and user testing to user flows, wireframes, prototyping, visual design, and business analysis. I align products with business and users. I believe a successful product is built with strong involvement from the people who will use it. I’m comfortable communicating with and managing stakeholders on both the user and business side, making sure their perspectives are part of the process.

I’m curious and constantly exploring new tools, technologies, and ways of working. Recently, I’ve been experimenting with AI as a way to expand my thinking and make my workflow more efficient, rather than replacing the creative and human side of design.

Give me a problem, a collaborative team, and something that hasn’t been figured out yet, and I’m in my element.
