CASE STUDY 03

Peoplebase, an AI-Enhanced Product Flow

Designing an HR platform through an AI-assisted design workflow.

A 3-day exploration of how AI can accelerate product design.

#01 [THE OUTCOME]
Combining product thinking, design systems, and AI-assisted workflows.

Peoplebase is an internal HR platform concept that demonstrates how AI and design systems can work together to accelerate product design while maintaining quality and consistency.

The project combines a scalable design system, AI-assisted ideation, and rapid prototyping into a complete product workflow.

#02 [WHY THIS PROJECT]
AI can generate interfaces.
But where does it fit into a designer's workflow?

Instead of treating AI as a replacement for design, I explored how it could become another design tool, helping generate ideas, speed up early design stages, and reduce repetitive work while keeping human judgment at the center.

#03 [MY APPROACH]

I started by building the foundation. The project followed three connected phases:

01

Creating the Basics

Create a scalable, lightweight design system using Variables, Tokens and reusable components in Figma.

02

Teaching AI

Connect AI to the design system so it understands the product language.

03

Building the Platform

Use AI to explore ideas with my integrated design system and create a coded platform to bring ideas to life.

#04 [CREATING THE FOUNDATION]
Every screen in the project is powered by one lightweight design system.

Instead of manually styling every interface, I created reusable foundations including:

Color Tokens Typography Variables Components Auto Layout Light/Dark Themes

This allowed new screens to be designed consistently while reducing repetitive work.

#05 [TEACHING AI MY DESIGN LANGUAGE]
A workflow where AI understands the design system instead of generating random interfaces.

I prompted Claude to review the design tokens, variables, components, variants, and their intended usage across the system. Based on this analysis, Claude generated reusable AI skills in a  SKILL.md file that documented how specific tokens and components should be applied.

These skills can be reused across multiple AI tools that can handle the .md file format (like Codex, ChatGPT, Cursor, or GitHub Copilot). In this way, instead of describing components repeatedly, all AI models could reference shared rules and produce much more consistent design suggestions.

#06 [BUILDING WITH AI]
AI became part of the design process, not the designer.

Throughout the project it helped me:

  • →explore layouts
  • →challenge UX decisions
  • →generate alternative solutions
  • →structure information architecture
  • →transition designs to a coded platform

Every screen was then reviewed manually inside Figma. Then Codex was used to create the coded version by pulling the designs from the Figma file.

#07 [CLAUDE × CODEX]

Different AI tools solved different problems.

ChatGPT

Used for early-stage ideation, brainstorming and sparring throughout the process.

Its strong reasoning and conversational capabilities enabled fast back-and-forth exploration, helping me challenge ideas and develop concepts efficiently.

Claude

Generated complete interface concepts and explored design directions, creating the first draft of the platform.

Its strong visual reasoning and ability to work directly with Figma made it ideal for early exploration, but its token usage was consumed quickly.

Codex

Refined existing screens, implemented targeted improvements, and accelerated iteration rounds.

With much lower token usage, it became the go-to tool for fast, repeated refinements throughout the design process.

Rather than relying on a single AI, I used each where it was strongest.

Source: www.morphllm.com/comparisons/codex-vs-claude-code

#08 [THE FINAL PRODUCT]
Everyday HR workflows, built on AI and a design system.

The final prototype focuses on everyday HR workflows while demonstrating how AI and design systems can support a modern product design process.

Rather than aiming for a final, releasable product, the project gave me space to explore how AI can become part of the design workflow.

#09 [WHAT I LEARNED]

This project demonstrated that thoughtful design foundations enable AI to become a more effective work partner for designers.

By combining a scalable design system with carefully structured prompts, AI became a faster collaborator while design decisions remained human.
